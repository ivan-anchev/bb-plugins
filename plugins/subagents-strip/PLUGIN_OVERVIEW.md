When an agent hands work to subagents, bb folds them into the thread's step list, and a background subagent reads as finished the moment it launches. This plugin adds a live strip above the composer that counts the latest prompt's subagents and child threads, plus any still running, and how long they have taken, and opens into one row per subagent that expands to its report and latest steps and opens its full transcript in the side panel or its own thread; a Subagents tab in the side panel lists every subagent in the thread, grouped by the prompt it was launched after.

## What you get

- **One line above the composer.** The provider's icon for each subagent, "4 subagents", then what needs attention, "3 running" ("5 done" once everything is), and the time of your latest prompt's subagents. It counts what the list shows: your latest prompt's subagents and any still running from an earlier one.
- **The rest, one click away.** Click the line to open the list above it. Its first row, "37 previous subagents · 1 failed · 1 stopped", counts everything the list leaves out and opens the Subagents tab; click "1 failed" or "1 stopped" to open the tab on that subagent, expanded. With nothing left out there is no such row.
- **Every subagent, by prompt.** The Subagents tab in the side panel lists the whole thread under its census ("41 subagents · 3 running · 1 failed · 1 stopped"; click a count to jump to its newest subagent), in batches, newest first. A batch is one of your prompts and every subagent launched after it until your next one, however many turns the agent took, including the ones it took on its own when a background agent finished and the ones a bb notice started. Each batch has a quiet label: its time, the first line of your prompt, and its counts ("1 running, 8 done"). The latest batch and any batch still running start open; older ones start closed and open with a click, and stay as you left them. A label stays at the top while its rows scroll under it. The earlier subagents' steps and reports are read when you open the tab.
- **One row per subagent.** Each row shows the provider's icon with a status dot, what the subagent was asked to do, what it is doing while it runs, in bb's words ("Running 7 commands, exploring 2 files"), or the first line of its report once it finishes, and how long it ran. Click again to close it.
- **A row opens in place.** Click a row to expand it, one at a time: when it started, how long it ran, how many steps it took, and, for background agents, its tool calls and tokens; then its report (six lines, with "Show more"), its error, or a note that it stopped; then its last six lines of work, folded the way bb's chat folds the same subagent ("Explored 3 files, ran 2 commands"), each opening to its steps, with "Show N earlier" above them. While it runs, the latest line shimmers, a group in the present tense ("Running 2 commands"), even for a background agent whose work bb's chat already shows as done, and the body stays on it. Esc closes the row.
- **Child threads too.** A bb thread started with this thread as its parent (`bb thread spawn --parent-thread`, or an agent spawning one) gets a row like any subagent: its title, its provider's icon, its latest step, and its closing message as its report once it settles. Its status is the thread's own: running while it works or waits on a question, done, failed with bb's error, or stopped, its time frozen at its stop. Tell it something more and the same row runs again and settles with the new reply. It settles in the strip even when it is set not to wake its parent.
- **The full transcript beside the chat.** "Open thread" on an expanded row, in the list or the Subagents tab, opens a side-panel tab named after the subagent, or switches to it if it is already open. It reads like bb's chat with the same subagent expanded: everything it said as markdown, and its work between messages folded into the same lines as the row ("Explored 3 files, ran 2 commands"), each opening to its steps: its thinking as a collapsed Thinking line, each command opening to its output, each file, edit, search, page, and tool call on one line. Its report or error comes at the end. It keeps growing while the subagent runs, its latest line in the present tense, and comes back after a reload. On a child thread, Open thread opens that thread itself.
- **Calm motion.** A row's text rolls to the new step and its dot changes colour; the running step shimmers. With reduced motion turned on, nothing shimmers and changes are instant.
- **The real status of background agents.** A background agent counts as running from launch until it finishes, even after the parent agent's turn has ended. Stop the thread and its agents read stopped, their time frozen at how long they ran; one that fails reads failed, with its error.
- **A child thread belongs to its batch**, by the turn that was under way when it was created.
- **A live clock.** The time is the latest batch's, from its first subagent's start to its last one's end. It ticks once a second while any of that batch's subagents runs.
- **Quiet when done.** When every subagent has finished, the strip dims until you point at it or open it.
- **Nothing when there is nothing.** A thread without subagents shows no strip, and bb's own "Running N background agents" bar stays as it is.

## How it works

The strip reads the thread's timeline, its background-task events, and its child threads and their timelines through bb's own API, in your browser, and updates when the thread or one of its children changes. It has no server logic, settings, commands, or network access beyond bb.

Status comes from Claude Code's background-task events. Other providers' subagents show the status bb's timeline gives them. A Codex subagent reads by its agent name and, once done, reports its last message; ACP subagents have not been checked live. Child threads of any provider read from bb's own thread status; a child's children and hidden or archived children are left out, and tokens show only for Claude Code background agents, the one source that reports them.
