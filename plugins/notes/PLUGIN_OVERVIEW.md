Experimental: it may break with a bb update.

Jot a note from anywhere in bb without leaving what you are doing. Click Notes in the sidebar footer or press ⇧⌘J: a Notes popover opens beside the sidebar with a one-line "New note…" field above your notes. Type, press ⌘↵, and the note lands at the top. Type @ to link a thread, and attach screenshots or recordings by pasting, dropping or clicking 📎. Or highlight text in a reply and choose Add to notes to keep that passage with a link back to its message. The same list is a Notes card on the New thread screen. Click a note to open it in place and read or delete it, or tick a few and choose Start thread to put them in the composer as context for a new thread.

## What you get

- **A Notes popover on every screen.** The Notes button in the sidebar footer, or ⇧⌘J (Ctrl+Shift+J off macOS), opens your notes beside the sidebar. The button and ⇧⌘J toggle it; Esc clears ticked notes and then closes it, and so does a click outside. Rebind the shortcut in Settings → Keyboard under "Plugin: notes · New note".
- **A one-line form that opens when you need it.** "New note…" rests as one line at the top; focus it and it opens with 📎, @ and Save, and stays open until the popover closes. ⌘↵ saves; the note lands at the top of the list with a short highlight, and the field is ready for the next one.
- **@ links to threads.** Type @ and pick one of your threads with ↑↓ and Enter: its title goes into the note and a chip shows in the form. Delete the title, or click the chip's ×, to unlink it.
- **Screenshots and recordings.** Paste a screenshot (⌘V), drop files on the form, or click 📎: each shows as a thumb with a × to remove it. PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, MP4, MOV and WebM, up to 100 MB each and 20 per note; HEIC and SVG are refused with a line saying why. A note can be just its files.
- **Passages from replies.** Highlight text in an assistant reply and choose **Add to notes** in the selection menu: the popover opens with the passage and a chip naming its thread, and ⌘↵ saves it, with or without a thought of your own. The chip opens the thread at that message. **Add to notes** in a message's action bar quotes the whole reply, or opens the form with no quote on your own message.
- **A Notes card on the New thread screen.** The same list as the popover, as tall as the card beside it and scrolling inside: each note on one line (a passage saved on its own shows in quotation marks), with the thread it came from under it, or the first thread it links ("No thread" for a note with neither). A note of files alone reads "2 screenshots", "1 recording" or "3 attachments", and a note's files show as a small fan of thumbs at the end of its row, "+N" beyond three. "New note" opens the popover's form from the card.
- **Notes open in place.** Click a note in the card or the popover and it opens under its row: the passage with its source chip, your words with each linked thread marked, and its media (videos play in place; an image opens full size, where ← → step through the note's files), with Open thread and **Delete**. Delete removes it for good with a "Note deleted" toast. Notes are not edited; delete one and write it again.
- **Start a thread from your notes.** Hover a note and tick its checkbox, and the footer reads "N selected · Clear" with **Start thread**. On the New thread screen it adds "Context from N notes:" to the composer after anything you already typed: each passage with a link to its thread and message, your words with their thread links, and the notes' images and videos as attachments (a file over bb's 35 MB attachment limit appears as its path on disk). From the popover on a thread page the button reads **Add to composer** and adds the same to that thread's draft. Review it and send it yourself. The notes you used leave the list; they are archived, not deleted, and there is no undo.

## How it works

Notes live in the plugin's own SQLite database on the bb server, and their files in a `media` folder beside it, so they survive reloads and restarts and never leave your machine. The plugin needs no account or setting.

The popover, the sidebar button, the @ picker and the card use bb surfaces that are still experimental, so a bb update can change or break them before the plugin catches up.
