Experimental: it may break with a bb update.

Jot a note from anywhere in bb without leaving what you are doing. Click Notes in the sidebar footer or press ⇧⌘J: a Notes popover opens beside the sidebar with your notes and a composer at its bottom. Type, press ⌘↵, and the note lands at the top. Type @ to link a thread, and attach screenshots or recordings by pasting, dropping or clicking 📎. Or highlight text in a reply and choose Add to notes to keep that passage with a link back to its message. The same list is a Notes card on the New thread screen. Click notes to tick them, then delete them, or choose New thread to put them in the new-thread composer as context, from any screen; on a thread page, Send to this thread puts them in that thread's draft instead. ⌘↵ and ⇧⌘↵ do the same from the list.

## What you get

- **A Notes popover on every screen.** The Notes button in the sidebar footer, or ⇧⌘J (Ctrl+Shift+J off macOS), opens your notes beside the sidebar. The button and ⇧⌘J toggle it; Esc closes the @ picker, then clears ticked notes, then closes it, and a click outside closes it too. Rebind the shortcut in Settings → Keyboard under "Plugin: notes · New note".
- **A composer that is always ready.** It sits at the bottom of the popover in bb's own composer style, with 📎, @ and a round send button, and grows upward as you write. ⌘↵ saves; the note lands at the top of the list with a short highlight, and the field is ready for the next one.
- **@ links to threads.** Type @ and pick one of your threads with ↑↓ and Enter: its title goes into the note and a chip shows in the form. Delete the title, or click the chip's ×, to unlink it.
- **Screenshots and recordings.** Paste a screenshot (⌘V), drop files on the form, or click 📎: each shows as a thumb with a × to remove it. PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, MP4, MOV and WebM, up to 100 MB each and 20 per note; HEIC and SVG are refused with a line saying why. A note can be just its files.
- **Passages from replies.** Highlight text in an assistant reply and choose **Add to notes** in the selection menu: the popover opens with the passage and a chip naming its thread, and ⌘↵ saves it, with or without a thought of your own. The chip opens the thread at that message. **Add to notes** in a message's action bar quotes the whole reply, or opens the form with no quote on your own message.
- **A Notes card on the New thread screen.** The same list as the popover, as tall as the card beside it and scrolling inside: each note on one line (a passage saved on its own shows in quotation marks), with the thread it came from under it, or the first thread it links ("No thread" for a note with neither). A note of files alone reads "2 screenshots", "1 recording" or "3 attachments", and a note's files show as a small fan of thumbs at the end of its row, "+N" beyond three. "New note" opens the popover's form from the card.
- **Click a row to tick it.** A click anywhere on a note's row, in the card or the popover, ticks it; another click unticks it, and dragging over its text to select it doesn't. Notes don't open and aren't edited: the row shows the note's first line and its thumbs, and New thread puts the whole note in the composer.
- **Delete from the ticks.** Tick notes and the footer reads "N selected · Clear", then **Delete** apart from **New thread ▾**. One click deletes those notes and their files for good, with a toast; there is no undo.
- **Start a thread from your notes.** Tick notes, and the footer reads "N selected · Clear" with **New thread**, or on a thread page a split button, **New thread ▾**. New thread adds "Context from N notes:" to bb's new-thread composer after anything you already typed: each passage with a link to its thread and message, your words with their thread links, and the notes' images and videos as attachments (a file over bb's 35 MB attachment limit appears as its path on disk). From a thread page or Tools it takes you to the New thread screen first. The ▾ menu also holds **Send to this thread**, which adds the same to the draft of the thread you are on. With focus in the Notes panel and outside a text field, ⌘↵ runs New thread and ⇧⌘↵ (or ⌥⌘↵) runs Send to this thread. Review the draft and send it yourself. The notes you used leave the list; they are archived, not deleted, and there is no undo.

## How it works

Notes live in the plugin's own SQLite database on the bb server, and their files in a `media` folder beside it, so they survive reloads and restarts and never leave your machine. The plugin needs no account or setting.

The popover, the sidebar button, the @ picker and the card use bb surfaces that are still experimental, so a bb update can change or break them before the plugin catches up.
